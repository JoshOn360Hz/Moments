FAQ

Does Moments sync across devices?

Moments stores data on-device. If a future version adds sync, you will be prompted and given control over the feature.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete moments inside the app at any time. Removing the app will also delete local data.

Do you use third-party ads?

No. Moments does not serve ads or use ad networks.
