Questions?

Does Moments sync across devices?

Moments stores data on-device by default. Future versions may add optional sync with your full control.

How do I delete my data?

Delete moments in the app anytime. Deleting the app removes all local data.

Do you collect any data?

No. We don't collect analytics, ads, or tracking data. Your privacy is respected by design.

Is there a web version?

Currently iOS only. We may expand to other platforms in the future.
